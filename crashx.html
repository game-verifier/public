CrashX - Game Verification Script

Third party script used to verify rounds of CrashX.

Provably Fair - Every CrashX round has a pre-generated SHA256 hash. The hashes form a chain: the previous round's hash is SHA256 of the next round's hash, so past results cannot be changed after they are published.

The crash point of a round is calculated from its hash and the salt:

  1. If the salt is not empty: h = HMAC_SHA256(message = the round's hash bytes, key = salt text); otherwise h = the round's hash
  2. Take the first 13 hex characters of h (52 bits) as an integer r
  3. Exact crash point C = 252 / (252 − r)
  4. Crash time crashAt = floor(ln(C) / 0.00006) ms after the round starts
  5. Bust = floor(e0.00006 × crashAt × 100) / 100, the multiplier the curve reached at that millisecond

No house edge is taken from the crash point. Paste a round's hash below to recalculate it and the rounds before it.
